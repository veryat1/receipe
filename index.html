Our Story

A kitchen built on family recipes

What started as a single stovetop and a notebook of handwritten recipes has grown into a restaurant that still cooks the same way: slowly, seasonally, and with ingredients we'd be proud to serve our own family. Every dish on our menu traces back to a memory, a market stall, or a Sunday dinner.
