Our Story

Family recipes, seasonal ingredients, and a dining room that feels like home.

Philosophy

Cooking the way it used to be done

Recipe Secrets began at a kitchen table, not in a boardroom. Our founder grew up watching recipes get passed from grandmother to mother to daughter — never written down exactly the same way twice, always tasting like home. We opened this restaurant to keep that tradition alive: dishes built on patience, good ingredients, and the belief that food should make people feel cared for.

Today our kitchen still sources from local farms, bakes bread daily, and simmers stocks and sauces for hours rather than minutes. Nothing here is rushed, and nothing is left to a shortcut when the long way tastes better.

Meet the Chef

Chef Daniel Cho

Daniel trained in family kitchens long before he ever trained in professional ones. After a decade cooking across coastal and countryside restaurants, he returned home to open Recipe Secrets — a menu built entirely from the recipes he grew up with, refined but never over-polished. He still makes the Sunday ragu himself.
